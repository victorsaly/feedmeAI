From an open fridge to a short shopping list.

  1. Photograph it

    The fridge with the door open, or everything on the worktop. The app lists what it can see, with rough amounts, and you correct anything it got wrong.

  2. Pick dinner

    Each idea says how much of your food it uses and what it still needs. Open one and the missing things come first, with one button to add them to your list. Then cook it a step at a time, with a timer where a step needs one and, if you like, a picture of each step.

  3. Buy the rest

    The list names what to look for on the shelf. Signed in, it finds the cheapest supermarket for all of it. UK shops only for now.
