# The product site: home page

Scope: `index.html` with `src/site/site.css`; `privacy/` and `support/` are reading pages in the same file's
styles. Mode: Persuade (home), Read (privacy, support). Code-led: no image generation was available.
Audience and job: someone who has not used FeedMe AI. They should understand in one screen that a photo of
their food becomes dinner and a short shopping list, and open the app or the sample.
Proof on hand: real screenshots of the app on the recorded sample, the sample photo, six licensed recipe
photos. No testimonials, ratings, user counts or press exist; none may be invented. The app is not on the App
Store, so there is no store badge; the film does not exist yet, so there is no video.
Unresolved: a support email address to publish; whether the privacy page's wording is what Victor wants.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page does what the app does. A real photo of food sits in the first screen with what the app read
in it stuck on as magnets, and the app's answer beside it. It refuses the app-landing default of a headline
over a floating phone and three icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: The app's own (DESIGN.md): cold enamel ground, white panels, Denim `#3667c9` for the one action,
the amber shelf tag for what is missing, Bricolage Grotesque for headings at display scale, the phone's own
sans for reading. Tilted pill magnets with a small lift. One full-width field of Denim, at the close.

STORY: They see food become a list of ingredients and dinners; learn the three steps from real screens;
learn it works offline, needs no account and does not keep the photo; see they can cook with no photo at
all; and open the app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1280 x 860: left, the question "What's in the fridge?" at about 6rem, one sentence,
"Open the app" (Denim) and "Try a sample photo" (white), one line of terms. Right, the sample photo as a tall
tilted print with six magnets across its lower half and a phone showing the app's result overlapping its
corner, the interior-light glow behind. Phone 390 x 844: the same question, sentence and two actions, with
the top of the photo entering the screen. Signature interaction: "Try a sample photo" opens the app already
showing that photo's result. Motion: the magnets snap on once, in order; nothing else moves.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). Pinned by the approved review page's site section ("hero, the film, the store
badge, how it works, privacy and support") and by the established world; the film and the badge are left out
because neither exists yet.
