Support

The quickest answers, then how to reach a person.

Using it

It read my photo wrong

Tap edit beside the ingredients. Pull off anything that is not there, add anything it missed, and tap an amount to change it. Then tap Update ideas. A photo in good light, with labels facing you, reads best.

It says I have used today's photos

Reading a photo costs money to run, so there is a daily limit. It resets the next day. The sample photo, your earlier photos and the built-in recipes are not counted and still work.

I have no signal

Your shopping list, saved recipes, earlier photos and the built-in recipes work with no connection once the app has been opened once. A new photo needs a connection.

Where are shop prices?

On the List tab, once you are signed in: Find the cheapest shop. Only UK supermarkets have been tested. Prices come from shop listings at the moment you ask and can change before you get there.

Can I put it on my phone's home screen?

Yes. Open the app in your phone's browser. On an iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. An iPhone app is on the way.

Recipes

A recipe looks wrong or unsafe

The recipes made from your photo are written by an AI and can be wrong. At the foot of every recipe there is Report this recipe. Choose a reason, add a note if you like, and send it. A person reads every report. If a recipe ever asks you to do something you would not normally do with food, don't.

The nutrition figures

They are estimates per serving, useful as a rough guide and not a measurement. Do not rely on them for a medical diet or an allergy.

Your account and data

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in only carries your list and saved recipes to another device, and unlocks shop prices and a wider recipe search.

How do I delete my data?

Open Account in the app and choose Delete my data. The privacy page says exactly what that removes.

Reach a person

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened; a screenshot helps. Please do not put anything private in an issue, as it is public.
