Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026. FeedMe AI is made by Victor Saly.

FeedMe AI works without an account and keeps as much as it can on your own device. This page says what leaves your device, where it goes, and how to remove it. There are no adverts and no analytics or tracking scripts in the app or on this site.

What stays on your device

  • Your shopping list and saved recipes, when you are not signed in.
  • Your recent photos, as small copies, with the ingredients and ideas that came from them.
  • Your recent recipe searches, the meal you last chose and the country you chose for shop prices.
  • A random number that identifies this device to our server, so your daily allowance is your own. It says nothing about you or the device.

All of this lives in your browser's storage for this site, or inside the app. Clearing the site's data, or deleting the app, removes it.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It uses the account service shared by victorsaly.com's apps, which signs you in with Google. FeedMe AI receives an account number and your name from that service; it does not receive your email address or your Google password.

Once signed in, your shopping list and saved recipes are stored on our server (a Cloudflare database) under your account number, so they can follow you to another device. A saved recipe is the recipe itself and, where it has one, a picture of the dish. Your own photos are never stored there.

How long things are kept

  • Photos: not stored by our server at all. OpenAI handles what it is sent under its own policies.
  • Your list and saved recipes: until you remove them or delete your data.
  • Daily use counts: a count per day against an account number, device number or network address.
  • Reports: kept so that recipes can be checked. A report made while signed in is detached from your account if you delete your data.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Account and choose Delete my data. It removes your shopping list, saved recipes and use counts from our server and from the device you are using, and signs you out.

It does not remove your sign-in with victorsaly.com itself, which is shared with its other apps. To have that removed as well, ask through the support page.

Children

FeedMe AI is not written for children and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go through the support page.
