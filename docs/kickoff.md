## The first deliverable: one visual, before any code

Victor wants to see the whole product on one page and approve it before anything is built. Make one page
(an Artifact he can open) with three parts:

1. **The app.** The key screens as they are today, from real screenshots on a phone, beside what they should
   become. This app is getting a new design, so show two or three directions for the look, not one.
2. **The website.** A mock of the product site that explains the app: the first screen, the video's place,
   the store badge, how it works, privacy and support. Say where the web app itself will live once the site
   takes the home page.
3. **Production.** A plain map of what stands between today and a live App Store listing: each step, its
   size, what blocks what, and the decisions that are Victor's (seller name, free or paid, Android).

Mark every assumption as an assumption. Where the audit could not look (every screen after the photo, and the signed-in list), look first, then draw.

## After Victor approves the visual

Follow the order in `docs/product-plan.md`. Keep two more files as the work goes, as DryRevival does: `docs/status.md`
(where it is today) and `docs/history.md` (each decision, with its date and why). The video is made last,
from the finished app.

## Do not

- Build the native shell before the web app is reviewed and finished.
- Change the app's own look to match another app. Each keeps its identity.
- Commit `docs/product-plan.md` or this file without Victor asking.
